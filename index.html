Trainable Telegraph-Inspired PDE Model for Image Denoising

Project: Learnable Hyperbolic PDE Image Denoiser, Mar 2026 · paper at CVIP 2026, final review round · third of five authors

A network built by unrolling a damped hyperbolic (telegraph-type) PDE into stages, so each stage conditions on two earlier iterates instead of one. The starting PDE came from our professor, Mahipal Jetta, and the equation comes from prior literature; the team turned it into a trainable network.

Result

Gain in mean PSNR over TNRD's official weights, averaged over five test sets: +1.23 dB at noise sigma 15, +1.17 dB at sigma 25 and +1.36 dB at sigma 50.

Team of four students Under Prof. Jetta; I am third of five authors. I set up the FoE400 training-data pipeline the team adopted. The follow-up work is separate and mine.

Follow-up: attention inside the PDE

Jun to Jul 2026 · builds on the paper, alongside the internship

I replaced each stage's learned filters with attention-based diffusion: first window attention, then a patch-similarity graph Laplacian. I also built a conservative-flux PDE variant, and ran depth sweeps, keeping a configuration only if its gain held across two random seeds. This is a known direction (attention-guided edge-stopping), not a new idea.

Mine The design, code and experiments, at Prof. Jetta's direction.

Team project Mine: evaluation, the prediction-head and loss ablations, and Grad-CAM analysis of the high-entropy failures.
